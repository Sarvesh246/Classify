# Classify

**Classify** is a web app for **course and professor planning**: grade outcomes, trends, instructor context, and tools to search, compare, and save what matters for your schedule.

---

## What it is

Classify brings together **official grade signals** (where available), **course and instructor discovery**, and **transparent scoring** so you can see *how confident* a ranking is—not just a single number with no context. Coverage varies by school; the app tells you when data is strong, partial, or still filling in.

---

## What you can do

- **Search** across schools, courses, departments, and professors from one place (with suggestions as you type).
- **Open a school** to explore courses, departments, trending picks, and the full instructor directory.
- **Inspect a course** for leaderboards and how sections have behaved over time when that data exists.
- **Open a professor profile** for grade patterns, course history, and review-style signals when published.
- **Compare** instructors or courses side by side in a dedicated workspace.
- **Save** shortlists and picks so you can come back without redoing the same search.
- **Plan “My courses”** at a school: build a multi-course list, sort it, and share it with a link.
- **Read Methodology** for how scores are built, how missing data is handled, and what the coverage badges mean.

---

## How to use the site

1. **Start at the home page** for national context, featured courses, and entry points into search and schools.
2. **Use Search** whenever you know part of a name, course code, or school—you can jump straight to the right hub or profile.
3. **Pick a school** from Search or the Schools path in the nav to see everything scoped to that campus.
4. **Trust the labels**: coverage badges and methodology copy explain whether you’re looking at rich institutional data, lighter evidence, or planner-only readiness.
5. **Use Compare** when you’re deciding between a few options; use **Saved** to keep a working list.

For the full story on numbers and evidence tiers, open **Methodology** in the app.
